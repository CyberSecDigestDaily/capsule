Capsule

Privacy

Short version: no passwords, no email address, no ads, no tracking. Your taste is yours.

Without an account

Everything you do (hearts, passes, your answers to the style questions, your sizes, your wardrobe) is saved in your browser on this device only. Nothing about you is sent to Capsule's server. Clearing your browser data, or You › Privacy and your data › Clear this device, removes it.

With an account

Accounts are made with a passkey, so Capsule never sees or stores a password, and never asks for your email. To keep your devices in step it stores:

  • the name you pick, and your passkeys' public keys (which can't be used to sign in anywhere on their own);
  • your reactions to pieces, your settings, your answers to the style questions, the sizes and measurements you enter, and the wardrobe you add;
  • signed-in devices as a coarse label (for example "Safari on iPhone") with when they were last used, so you can sign one out. Never your full browser details.

It's held in Cloudflare's database in the European Union, private to your account. It isn't sold, shared or used for advertising.

What isn't kept

  • IP addresses: to slow down abuse, sign-in attempts are counted against a one-way, keyed fingerprint of your address, never the address itself, and the counts are deleted within a day.
  • Analytics or tracking: there are none. The only cookie is the sign-in cookie, and only once you sign in.
  • Payment details: Capsule doesn't sell anything. Buying happens on the shops' own sites, under their privacy policies.

The shops

Product photos load straight from each shop's image servers, so those shops can see that a browser at your address loaded a photo, as with any website showing their pictures. Capsule asks browsers not to send which page you were on. Links to a piece take you to the shop's site.

How long things are kept

  • Sign-ins last 30 days from your last visit, and never longer than 180 days.
  • Your reactions, settings and answers stay until you change or delete them, or delete your account.
  • Single-use codes (linking a device, recovery, invites) are deleted once used or expired.

Your choices

All of this is self-serve under You › Privacy and your data: Export everything downloads all your data as a file; Delete account removes your account and everything in it straight away; Clear this device wipes what's saved in this browser. For anything else, contact the person who invited you.
